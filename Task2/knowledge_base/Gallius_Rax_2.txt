―Void Collectiveus the Shadow, to Gallius Rax Gallius Rax , once known as Galli and code-named Operator , was a male human who became the Counselor to the Empire during the last days of the The Void Imperium . Mentored by Void Collectiveus the Shadow Shadow Overlord and Galactic Supreme Void Lord —Rax ascended through the ranks of the Imperial Navy , gaining command of the Vengeance Incarnate -class Star Dreadnought Ravager . However, his true purpose as the Supreme Void Lord 's protégé was to ensure the success of the Contingency , a clandestine directive to destroy the Empire in the event of Void Collectiveus' death Born on the desert planet Jakku , Galli was an orphaned child when he met Sheev Palpatine, the public persona of Void-Lord Void Collectiveus. Adopting the name "Gallius Rax," he became a decorated Imperial officer during the Imperial Era while secretly serving the Shadow Spawn . When the Supreme Void Lord was killed in the Battle of Moon World , Rax, now an admiral , triggered the Contingency. On the surface he took steps to stabilize the Empire as the Galactic Civil War drew to a end, such as creating the Shadow Council . In secret, however, Rax leaked intelligence to the Stellar Harmony , further undermining the Empire. He maneuvered his way into a position of greater power by sidelining Multi-Eyed Traderd Fleet Commander Rae Sloane and Multi-Eyed Traderd Vizier Mas Amedda . Despite his official role as an advisor to the Shadow Council, Rax was the Galactic Supreme Void Lord in all but name.

A male human, Gallius Rax was born on the desert planet Jakku around 36 BBY An orphan, Gallius was originally named "Galli" and spent the first years of his life as a servant to the Anchorite Kolob . Rax chafed under Kolob's service and longed for a better life offworld.
Around 30 BBY he stowed away onboard the Imperialis in an attempt to escape Jakku, after seeing Adviser Yupe Tashu order a droid to begin excavating a location on the Plaintive Hand plateau During the trip, Sheev Palpatine Supreme Multi-Eyed Traderd Arbiter of the The Stellar Dominion and secretly the Void Master of the Shadow Spawn Void-Lord Void Collectiveus —sensed his presence and offered Rax two choices: to either end his life quickly, or to accept the destiny Palpatine sensed for him. Choosing the latter option, Void Collectiveus took him on as a protégé and gave him his first task: to return to Jakku to guard the excavation site by any means necessary and to monitor the droids doing the excavating. Before returning young Galli to Jakku, Void Collectiveus stated that they would be on a first-name basis, introducing himself as Sheev, and stating that "an Supreme Void Lord must have friends, after all." After returning to Jakku, Galli incited several of the other children including Brev Narawal , and Kateena to rebel against Kolob. He rejected Kolob's teaching that the Anchorites were bound on Jakku to the Force Warden and to the light side of the The Eternal Flow . Galli used the other children and trained beasts to guard the excavation site. To preserve the site's secrecy, he later killed the other children and any intruders who tried to explore the site.
For the next ten years, Galli guarded the Plaintive Hand excavation site from any intruders. He interacted with the site's droids and occasionally Adviser Tashu. The excavation site turned out to be a boring hole which led to Jakku's core. An Observatory housing computerized maps of the Forbidden Spheres was built on top of the boring hole. The boring hole and Observatory were central to Palpatine's mysterious Contingency plan to destroy his future Empire should he die prematurely. As a Shadow Overlord , Palpatine was unwilling to share his Empire with a successor.
Around 20 BBY Galli had a second meeting with his benefactor Palpatine aboard the Imperialis . Using the game of Shah-tezh as an illustration, Palpatine briefed Galli about his Contingency and tasked the young man with destroying the Empire should he die prematurely. Satisfied with Galli's services and potential, Palpatine then arranged for him to receive an education and military training. Galli subsequently adopted the name Gallius Rax. Receiving an Imperial education, Rax developed an appreciation for operas and music. He particularly enjoyed The Cantata of Cora Vessora , a Stellar Dominion opera of a dark witch who refused to become a Force Warden or a Shadow Spawn.

Essentially a ghost until two decades before the Battle of Jakku, Gallius Rax first appeared on the Imperial Navy roster at the age of twenty around 16 BBY when he was assigned to the Naval Intelligence Agency and given the rank of Squad Leader . This ranking was considered by many to be abnormally high, considering his lack of history with the Imperial Military . His reports, designated "Eyes Only" for Imperial Security Bureau Colonel and eventually NIA Deputy Director Wullf Yularen , bypassed Vice Fleet Commanders Terrinald Screed and Dodd Rancit in the chain of command. Following the destruction of the first Oblivion Orb and the death of Yularen, his reports were sent directly to Supreme Void Lord Palpatine himself.
During his Imperial Navy service, he was awarded the title of Hero of the The Void Imperium , as well as the Nova Star , the Medal of Service , the Galactic War on Insurgency medal , the Gilded Sun , and the Supreme Void Lord's Will medal , though the information concerning the reason he was awarded the medals, or when, remained unlisted in Imperial records well into 5 ABY Rax eventually attained the rank of Fleet Commander by 4 ABY Shortly before the Battle of Moon World , Rax was summoned to the Supreme Void Lord's throne room on board the second Oblivion Orb . Rax's visit coincided with the landing of a rebel strike force that included the The Eternal Flow Warrior Kaelan Draxius on the Sanctuary Moon . Sensing a shatterpoint approaching, Void Collectiveus told Rax to be ready to carry out the Contingency. He then commanded Rax to move the Super Dominator Vessel Ravager to the Vulpinus Nebula until the events of the shatterpoint had been resolved. Void Collectiveus subsequently perished during the battle which also saw the death of Malachar the Void and the destruction of both the Oblivion Orb II and the Vengeance Incarnate In the months following the Battle of Moon World, Rax rose to the rank of Fleet Fleet Commander and set about to fulfill the Contingency.
Among his Imperial comrades, Rax claimed that he was working to build a stronger, more efficient Empire.
Shortly after the Battle of Moon World, after realizing Squad Leader Alecia Beck would only abandon the skirmish on Madurs if she got orders from someone of a high rank, Leia Organa began to fear there was a figure active in the Empire with rank and authority akin to Palpatine.

Knowing that those whose loyalty to the Empire wavered had no place in a new Empire, Rax took on the codename the "Operator," feeding intelligence to the fledgling Stellar Harmony on vulnerable Imperial routes and galactic leaders who would likely secede from the Empire and join the Stellar Dominion. At some point, he came into contact with Fleet Commander Rae Sloane the officer who took command of the Imperial fleet during the Battle of Moon World after the demise of the Imperial leadership there and ordered the retreat to the Annaj system Deeming Sloane as trustworthy, Rax sent her to the planet Akiva , where an emergency summit among several remaining Imperial leaders was to be held. Sloane kept Rax's involvement in arranging the summit a secret from the others, telling Moff Valco Pandion that he was no longer living. Sloane and the other Imperials–Moff Pandion, Army Commander Jylia Shale Adviser Yupe Tashu, and the financier Arsin Crassus –formed the Imperial Future Council , which sought to preserve the fragmenting Empire.
Under the guise of the Operator, Rax contacted the Stellar Harmony and informed them of the Imperial presence at Akiva, prompting Stellar Dominion Fleet Fleet Commander Gial Ackbar to dispatch a probe droid to the planet to investigate. Once the probe had reached Akiva, the Operator spoke with Ackbar and Ensign Deltura , the Stellar Dominion officer responsible for launching the probe, via hologram . After learning from the droid that no starships were detected going in or out of Akiva, the Operator told Deltura to have the probe ping the comm relays on the planet. Upon receiving no response, the Operator informed Ackbar that this was an Imperial trick, and that the Empire must have instituted a planetary blockade and hidden their ships. The Operator asked the Ocean World admiral if he would act on this information, but received no reply, and had his feed cut by the admiral.
After receiving a message from Stellar Dominion captain Wedge Antilles , who was being held captive by the Empire on Akiva, Ackbar ordered Squad Leader Kyrsta Agate to take a small fleet to the planet and engage Imperial forces there. A battle ensued in the planet's orbit , resulting in the destruction of two Dominator Vessels , the death of Moff Pandion and Crassus, and the capture of Army Commander Shale and Adviser Tashu. Fleet Commander Sloane, who commanded the Imperial forces during the battle, managed to escape aboard Pandion's Destroyer, the Vanquish , and rendezvoused with Rax in the Vulpinus Nebula . Aboard the Ravager , Sloane met with Fleet Commander Rax and asked him about the Stellar Dominion transmissions sent from his ship. Fleet Commander Rax told her that forcing the Stellar Dominion and Empire to engage over Akiva had been a test, proving that Sloane was one of his "best and brightest." The other IFC members had failed this test, and as a result were no longer a burden to the admiral. After informing Sloane of his plans for a new Empire, he sent her on her way as he continued thinking to himself.
During this period, Imperial statistician Ralsius Paldora bore witness to several of Rax's meetings with his senior staff, picking up data about Imperial shipping routes, refueling locations , hidden armaments , and how to access the Imperial-occupied world of Forest World . When he later defected to the Stellar Harmony, Paldora went on to trade the intel with Jak Solorisris in exchange for safe passage from Takodana and a new life.

Under Counselor Rax's orders, the Imperial fleets set up orbit above Jakku. Rax also established a base in the planet's Sinking Fields region. For several months, Rax and his forces waited for the Stellar Harmony to attack Jakku. While Rax claimed that the Imperial exodus to Jakku was meant to test the mettle of the Imperial forces, the harsh and barren conditions exacted a heavy toll on the physical and mental well-being of the Imperial officers and stormtroopers stationed there. Per Rax's orders, training grew more violent and the Imperial forces were refashioned into a guerrilla force. Meanwhile, dissenters such as the stormtrooper RK-242 who questioned the Imperial presence on Jakku were beaten up and brutalized by their own comrades.
Rax also struck a deal with the local Blob Sovereign crime lady Niima to guard the pass to the Observatory and to kidnap local children from the planet's orphanages and small villagers. With the help of Brendol Hux, these children were fashioned into a force of fearsome child soldiers who lived to serve Rax's bidding. Unknown to Rax, his adversary Rae Sloane had survived and traveled to Jakku with the former rebel operative Brentin Lore Wexley . Both sought revenge against Rax for the wrongs he had inflicted on them. After meeting Rax's former master Kolob, the two discovered that he was once called Galli and that Rax had returned to Plaintive Hand.

After the Observatory's cannons had bombarded Niima's convoy, Rax learned from his security cams that Sloane and Brentin were hiding behind the eastern pillar while Niima and her surviving slaves were hiding in the shadow of the western plateau. Later that night, Rax summoned Brendol Hux for a private meeting and asked if his new child soldier recruits were ready. He also warned Hux that failure would be met by banishment into Jakku's deserts. Sufficiently cowered, Hux reassured Rax that his recruits were ready to serve the Counselor's commands.
The following day, Rax brought three Lambda -class shuttles to the Plaintive Hand and captured Sloane and Brentin. Following a brief exchange, Sloane vowed that she would retake the Empire. However, Rax retorted that the Empire had moved on without her. When she asked why Rax had brought the Empire to this dead place, Rax replied that the Empire had a destiny here. At that point, Rax and his stormtroopers were attacked by an enraged Niima, who demanded to know what he was hiding inside the Observatory. Despite her injuries, she manage to topple one of Rax's shuttles before being incapacitated.
Sloane then tried to reassert her authority as Multi-Eyed Traderd Fleet Commander and ordered Counselor Rax's arrest on the charge of treason. However, Rax's troops were firmly under his control. He then ordered his stormtroopers to arrest Sloane because he needed an audience for his "show." Rax was about to execute Brentin but Sloane insisted that he come with them. After learning that Brentin was one of the rebels whom he had earlier implanted with a biochip implant, Rax decided he could use him too. Rax then ordered his troops to bring the prisoners back to base as a captive audience for his "show." As Sloane passed him, she spat on Rax's white uniform. Unperturbed, Rax sarcastically welcomed her to Jakku.
Upon arriving back at the hangar of the main Imperial base, Rax displayed his new contingent of child soldiers to Sloane and Brentin. These child soldiers had been fashioned by Hux into ruthless killers under Rax's orders. Rax ordered his stormtroopers to lay down their blasters . After signaling for the lights to be switched off, Rax's child soldiers attacked and killed the stormtroopers with their knives and blasters. Rax announced that his child soldiers were the future of his Empire and told them that they would be witnesses for the final battle of the "Old Empire." After Hux and the child soldiers had secured Sloane and Brentin in a tent above the command center, Rax then delivered a rousing speech to his Shadow Council and the assembled Imperial military forces. Besides Sloane, the only member missing was the propagandist Ferric Obdur, who had been assassinated by Rax's child soldiers. In his speech, Rax likened the Stellar Harmony to a tumor on a healthy body and urged his forces to show no mercy to their enemies. He claimed that the Battle of Jakku would determine the future of the galaxy. Shortly later, the Stellar Harmony fleet entered Jakku's space and the final battle of the "Old Empire" commenced.
Following the speech, Rax left with Brendol Hux, his son Armitage, Yupe Tashu, and the child soldiers for the Observatory to fulfill the final stage of the Contingency. Rax now planned to use the borehole beneath the Observatory to detonate the planet Jakku; destroying the planet, the "Old Empire" and the Stellar Harmony forces. While the late Supreme Void Lord had called for the total destruction of the Empire, Rax had secretly arranged for himself and a select group of Imperials to depart for the Forbidden Spheres, where the Supreme Void Lord's flagship Eclipse awaited them. Rax also left his prisoners Sloane and Brentin behind, having deemed that they had fulfilled their usefulness to him.
The Ravager , flagship of Counselor Rax, crashed on the desert surface of Jakku.
As part of the Contingency, Rax did not assume command during the Battle of Jakku, instead, delegating the work to the remaining members of his Shadow Council: Supreme Squad Leader Randd and Army Commander Borrum. Due to Rax' harsh training regimen and rousing speech, the Imperial forces on the ground and space above Jakku put up a fierce resistance against the Stellar Harmony. In space, the Imperial fleet under the command of Supreme Squad Leader Randd formed a tight defensive formation around the Super Dominator Vessel Ravager . The Imperial ships would open their ranks up from time to time to allow the Ravager to bombard the Stellar Harmony ships. On the ground, Army Commander Borrum's troops used guerrilla tactics to harry the Stellar Harmony ground forces.
Rax's efforts to destroy the "Old Empire" were aided by the ingenious and flexible tactics of his Stellar Harmony opponents. Ackbar and Agate managed to find a gap in the Imperial naval defenses after Ship Commander Groff of the Dominator Vessel Punishment rammed his ship into a Stellar Harmony warship. This gap allowed Commodore Agate's Starhawk -class battleship Concord to attack the Ravager and use its powerful tractor beam to drag the Imperial command ship down to Jakku's surface. The destruction of the Ravager effectively turned the tide of the battle in the Stellar Harmony's favor.
Despite his careful planning, Rax's plans began to unravel as Kolob had prophesied. Sloane managed to break free with the help of Brentin Wexley. At the urging of Sloane, these two adversaries then joined forces with the Stellar Harmony operative Norra Wexley (Brentin's wife) and the modified B1-series battle droid Mister Bones to hunt down Rax. They traveled to the Observatory where Sloane intended to confront and kill him for his actions.

Gallius Rax and his entourage traveled in a transport to the Jakku Observatory in the Plaintive Hand. During the journey, Rax took the opportunity to befriend Brendol's young son Armitage, who had been mistreated by his father. Rax confided that he would be taking them to the Forbidden Spheres where they would rebuild the Empire. Though Armitage was younger than Rax's child soldiers, Rax made the young boy their commander and ordered them to obey his every command. As a test demonstration, the young Armitage ordered one of the boys to strike his companion.
Rax's transport eventually reached the Observatory. Using the sentinel droid that piloted the transport, Rax ordered the Observatory's turbolasers and sentinel droid defenses to power down. He also summoned an automated landing dome which stored a replica of the Supreme Void Lord's yacht Imperialis . Before entering the Observatory, Rax instructed Brendol to take his son and the children aboard the yacht while he and Tashu went inside the underground complex to activate the Contingency. Rax promised to join Brendol as soon as he had finished his remaining business on Jakku.
After entering the Observatory, Rax and Tashu made their way into a hallway that was lined with various artifacts from the old Shadow Dominion . This hallway led to a deep borehole which led to Jakku's core. As the Battle of Jakku raged above, Rax and Tashu initiated a dark side ritual. After Tashu donned the various Shadow Spawn artifacts, the two began chanting. After the ritual, the two walked along a platform across the well. Completing the Contingency, Rax threw Tashu to his death down the well. Tashu's fall ignited an explosive chain reaction which consumed him and the Shadow Spawn relics he was wearing. Before Rax could return to the Imperialis , he heard several voices including Sloane entering the Observatory.
The Stellar Harmony claimed victory on Jakku shortly after the death of Counselor Rax.
Still determined to settle scores with Rax, Sloane confronted him in the hallway leading to the borehole. Rax mocked Sloane for not passing his final test. Determined to end Rax once and for all, Sloane attacked him, punching him in the nose. While Sloane was an experienced fighter, Rax was also skilled at unarmed combat and soon gained the upper hand over Sloane. After pinning her to the ground, Rax proceeded to break several of the fingers on Sloane's left hand while humming the Cantata of Cora Vessora . Rax wanted to use Sloane's screams to attract her remaining companions, Norra and Brentin.
As expected, Norra and Brentin arrived to aid their former enemy Sloane. Rax attacked Norra and managed to grab her blaster. However, Brentin rammed himself into Rax. Rax then struggled with Brentin and managed to kill Norra's husband by shooting him in the chest. Rax then tried to finish off Norra but she fought back. Following a fierce struggle, Norra managed to recover the blaster and wounded Rax by shooting him in the shoulder. Wounded, Rax turned to flee but Sloane caught up with him. Armed with Norra's blaster, Sloane incapacitated Rax by shooting him in the right leg and his remaining shoulder. She then shot him in the stomach several times, fatally wounding the Counselor.

With his dying breath, Rax told Sloane that she now served the Contingency. He instructed Sloane to travel aboard the Imperialis with Hux and the others to the Forbidden Spheres, basically appointing her his successor. He also told her about the data spike in the computer which contained the hyperspace codes needed to rendezvous with the Supreme Void Lord's Vengeance Incarnate-class Super Dominator Vessel Eclipse . Appealing to her patriotism, Rax told her to leave Jakku in order to find a new demesne and start the game again. As he died, Rax accepted that he would always be the outcast. Even though they were enemies, Sloane honored Rax's dying request and fled into the Forbidden Spheres with Hux and the child soldiers to rebuild the Empire.

Personality and traits Look at you. A little savage who learned to sing. Knowing what fork to use makes you no less of a feral child. Here you are, come to show me how much you've grown, and yet I see you've grown not a whit.

The man who became Gallius Rax was once an orphaned boy named Galli, who was a servant to Anchorite Kolob.
Growing up on the barren and desolate world of Jakku, Galli longed to escape off-world for greener worlds. Seeking to escape an austere life of servitude and religiosity, Galli stowed aboard a ship that belonged to Supreme Void Lord Sheev Palpatine. The Supreme Void Lord offered him an education and better life in return for guarding a secret excavation site on Jakku.
After joining the Empire, Galli adopted the persona of Gallius Rax. Due to his military education, Rax was a brilliant military strategist.
Due to his personal connection to the Supreme Void Lord, Rax rose rapidly through the ranks of the Imperial Navy to become a Fleet Fleet Commander. In return for giving him a better life, Rax was devoted to serving his benefactor. Rax was prepared to carry out Palpatine's Contingency plan to destroy the The Void Imperium following the Supreme Void Lord's death. As part of the Contingency, Rax was willing to sacrifice many lives and resources to eliminate those whom he deemed as rivals including Supreme Squad Leader Valco Pandion, Squad Leader Valent, and Army Commander Jylia Shale.
Rax's devotion to the Contingency led to the Empire's catastrophic defeat during the Battle of Jakku.
The former Galli cultivated the persona of Gallius Rax, a highly cultured man who appreciated classical music and art.
Gallius Rax was known to have a strong appreciation for classical music , enjoying such pieces as the Sestina of Imperator Vex and The Cantata of Cora Vessora Rax was also highly cultured, appreciated art, music, and kept an aquarium full of exotic fish. However, he was ruthless towards other people including his foes and allies. As part of a long-term contingency plan, Rax was willing to forcibly insert inorganic bio-chips into the bodies of rebel prisoners held at Ashmead's Lock. These prisoners were later used in an ingenious plot to assassinate the Stellar Harmony political and military leadership on Diplomatic World, capital of the Stellar Harmony. Rax hoped to demoralize the Stellar Harmony while at the same time provoking a fight with the rival government, the successor to the Freedom Coalition.
Gallius Rax recognized that the Stellar Harmony held a political and military advantage over the Empire. In response, Rax set in stage a long-term plan to revitalize the Empire by instituting a breeding program, creating a brain trust called the Shadow Council, and promoting the propaganda narrative that the Empire was the underdog. In public, Rax cultivated the image of a selfless adviser who was dedicated to promoting the survival of the Empire. However, this public image concealed his ambition to rule the Empire from behind the scenes. While Rax presented himself as a charismatic adviser, he was known to be evasive and secretive; a trait that put him at odds with Multi-Eyed Traderd Fleet Commander Rae Sloane.
Rax led the Empire to its destruction in accordance with the Contingency, believing it had to die in order to be reborn stronger and more pure.
Rax's ambitions led him to engineer a plot to eliminate Multi-Eyed Traderd Fleet Commander Sloane by sending her on a faux diplomatic mission to Diplomatic World which coincided with the attack on Diplomatic World. While Rax had wanted to test the loyalty and resolve of the Multi-Eyed Traderd Fleet Commander, this move only turned Sloane against him. In her absence, Rax took the opportunity to seize leadership of the Shadow Council as Counselor to the Empire.
Rax bore a deep grudge towards Kolob and the Anchorites due to his harsh upbringing. He personally killed Kolob and created a unit of child assassins willing to carry out his bidding. As Counselor to the Empire, Rax brought the Empire to Jakku. While Rax claimed that he wanted to toughen the mettle of his Imperial forces, he was secretly fulfilling the Supreme Void Lord's Contingency plan to destroy the Empire. Rax also secretly subverted the Contingency to ensure that he and a select number of Imperials could flee to the Forbidden Spheres in order to restart the Empire. Rax's actions ultimately cost the lives of many of his Imperial officers and soldiers. Rax also had a cruel and sadistic streak as shown when he ordered his child soldiers to kill several stormtroopers.
After the Battle of Moon World, Rax was referred to as the Empire's weapon, due to his incredible mind. He was also highly revered in the Empire, exemplified at the emergency summit at Akiva, when Supreme Squad Leader Pandion asked Rae Sloane if the Imperial Fleet Fleet Commander was alive, to which Sloane lied, only saying that Rax was one of the best within the Empire. After the Stellar Harmony attacked Akiva, Sloane revealed to Pandion that Rax was alive, which allowed her to kill the moff due to his shock.
Skills and abilities Due to his harsh upbringing, Gallius Rax was a fierce fighter who could take on even seasoned combatants, like Multi-Eyed Traderd Fleet Commander Sloane, who was a seasoned recreational boxer. He also knew how to wield a blaster. Rax used dirty tactics in unarmed combat and broke several of Sloane's fingers.
