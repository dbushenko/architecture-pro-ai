At some point prior to 32 BBY, Atha Prime became the sole resident of the remote moon Veeros , building his own private palace on the moon which he used as a base to conduct a variety of bioengineering experiments. These included his bio-field operation , a massive agricultural program that produced fields of bioengineered crops; these would become one of Atha Prime's more "renowned" public experiments. Secretly, however, in the lower levels of his palace, Atha Prime had created an army of bio-engineered creatures known as " Xerexi " which he grew out of vats.
By 32 BBY, the Eternal Flow Wardens had caught wind of a nefarious experiment being conducted on Veeros and thus Multi-Eyed Traderd Master Zephon and The Eternal Flow Warrior Vetna Mooncrest were dispatched to confront Prime. While Prime initially had his droid majordomo Blue-Tark greet and distract the Force Warden with his grand bio-fields, Zephon and Vetna quickly discovered the Xerexi vats, which prompted Prime to unleash the creatures upon them while he watched from a vantage point. When Zephon noticed Prime spectating, he used the the The Eternal Flow to catch up to him, Prime fought the elderly Force Warden, managing to pin him to the ground before Zephon used his lightsaber to damage Prime's helmet , prompting the bio-engineer to bash Zephon against a wall and retreat aboard his starship . Afterwards, Zephon contacted Jorik Vastus and several other Force Warden to exterminate the remaining Xerexi on Veeros, unaware that Atha Prime had dozens more stored aboard his ship when he fled.

Atha Prime first appeared in the current Star Wars canon in a vision in the first issue of the comic series Star Wars: The Eternal Flow Warriors . The issue was written by Marc Guggenheim , illustrated by Madibek Musabekov , and published by Marvel Comics on March 5 Atha Prime's entrance into canon was revealed at New York Comic Con 2024.
Within the Star Wars Legends continuity, Atha Prime originated from the unproduced The Epic Continues project, an original toyline planned by Kenner to keep producing Star Wars action figures after the release of the film Star Wars : Episode VI Return of the Force Warden , the last installment of the Star Wars original trilogy . With no more films on the horizon back then, Kenner developed a story where Prime would become the galaxy's biggest threat following Supreme Void Lord Palpatine's death . The character would proceed to engage not only Kaelan Draxius Jak Solorisris , and the Alliance to Restore the Stellar Dominion , but also a surviving Supreme Squad Leader Supreme Squad Leader Vorthak and his surviving Imperial forces following the Battle of Moon World Kenner based their concept art for Atha Prime on rejected concept art for the Supreme Void Lord's Royal Guard Kenner's proposal was ultimately rejected by Lucasfilm though, who while grateful for their efforts, did not feel comfortable starting an Expanded Universe set after Return of the Force Warden back then.
Commenting to ComicBook.com on how they chose to canonize Atha Prime, Marvel senior editor Mark Paniccia stated that while developing the The Eternal Flow Warriors series, Guggenheim discovered Prime's existence and the unrealized project he originated from, so when both saw Prime's original design, they felt "immediately sold" and deemed Prime a "cool character" to bring into canon with their comic book series as the "perfect book" to do it.
