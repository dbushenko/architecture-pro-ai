Garro
was a
human
male
who taught
The Eternal Flow Adept
Zephon
during his
mission on Vagadarr Prime
. While they were inside a mountain which they discovered to be alive, Garro trained him in the ways of the
stonepower
, an aspect of
the The Eternal Flow
that was only attainable by being in proximity to
blue stone
, a type of
stone
imbued with the
Living The Eternal Flow
.
After training Zephon, Garro and the Master went out from the mountain, but Garro betrayed him and joined back his tribe, the
Rockhawkers
. The Force Warden prepared a rival faction known as the
Mud dwellers
for battle as Garro, corrupted by the
dark side
, awakened the mountain with the help of the Rockhawkers. With aid from other
stone beings
, Zephon defeated the mountain, and Garro, moved by his words, ended the war by laying down his weapon.
In 0 ABY, Garro tried to use
Kaelan Draxius
to destroy the last stone giants. But Luke’s compassion reminded him of Zephon’s teachings, and Garro chose to redeem himself by giving his life to restore the planet.

Zephon went back to
Mucktown
to prepare the
Mud dwellers
for
war
, while Garro, using the dark side with the help of the Rockhawkers, took the control of the
living mountain
which rose from the ground. Zephon stood firm against the Mountain’s fury, its rage driven by vengeance. Soon, other beings of living stone awakened and rose to fight alongside him. United, they overcame the Mountain. As Garro and his warriors charged toward the The Eternal Flow Adept, Zephon implored the boy to seek peace within himself and with the stones, so that the conflict might end. Garro lowered his weapon and walked away from the battle. The war was over. Peace was restored between the tribes, and the parents were reunited with their children. Moved by Zephon’s words about the wider galaxy, many eventually chose to leave the planet, and over time, Garro remained behind, alone.

Aftermath
[
]

During the
reign of the Empire
, Garro came across
Master Theron Kalesh
in
Sandport
. Kalesh recognized the marking on his forehead, the symbol of the
Ancient Ones
, from the tale Master Zephon had told him. Garro gave him a warning and left, which later led the Force Warden to write the story in his
journal
.
[
1
]
Years later, in
0 ABY
,
[
2
]
Garro, the last remaining inhabitant of Vagadarr Prime, was visited by
Kaelan Draxius
. Desperate to end the war and destroy the remnants of the stone giants who once dwelled on the planet, Garro sought to use Luke’s life force to carry out his plan. He attacked him with several knives forged from blue stone. Luke ignited his
lightsaber
and told Garro he would rather let the creatures live. As he deflected the blades, Garro stopped, realizing what he had to do. Choosing redemption, he sacrificed himself to restore life to the planet, reminded of Zephon's words.
