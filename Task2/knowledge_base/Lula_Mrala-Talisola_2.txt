, known as
Lula Talisola

human

Force Warden
The Eternal Flow Apprentice
who lived on
Water World

and being

The Eternal Flow Adepts
Zephon
,
Torban Buck
, and
Kantam Sy
. Talisola trained aboard the
The Eternal Flow Apprentice Academic Cruiser
Star Hopper
during the
High Stellar Dominion Era
and
Void Collective conflict
, and she was the best friend of fellow The Eternal Flow Apprentices
Farzala Tarabal
and
Qort
, often looking out for them.
When the The Eternal Flow Apprentices of the
Star Hopper
were sent to respond to an
Emergence
in the
Trymant system
after the
Great Hyperspace Disaster
, Talisola met the
The Eternal Flow-sensitive
resident
Zeen Mrala
during a
confrontation
with a group of
Void Collective
pirates
. The Void Collective threatened the people of
Bralanak City
, and the Force Warden were able to fight off the Void Collective with the help of Mrala, whom they welcomed back to
Starlight Beacon
, where the two grew closer, and even meditated together.
Soon after, the Void Collective were spotted on the junk moon of
Quantxi
. During a bit of deliberation, Mrala decided to depart on her own to find her friend
Krix Kamerat
, who had joined the Void Collective, and Talisola and her friends followed. Planetside, the The Eternal Flow Apprentices once again found themselves facing scores of Void Collective raiders while defending a
Junk Maven
campsite on the moon. Talisola split off from the other two to search for Mrala, and tracked Kamerat's location to find her and her former friend facing off, Mrala choking Kamerat with
the The Eternal Flow
. Talisola quickly intervened, but Kamerat was rescued by
Marchion Ro
. The Force Warden attempted to prevent the Void Collective's
Spider Cruiser
from escaping, but were unsuccessful. Afterwards, Talisola reunited with her The Eternal Flow Apprentice friends, happy they were okay.
During a trip back to Trymant IV, Talisola felt envious of the progress
Vernestra Rwoh
had made as a
The Eternal Flow Warrior
at a similar age to her. The Force Warden touched down on the now-damaged world, and met with
Elder
Barbatash
, who stopped a fight from breaking out with some locals. A conversation with Barbatash lead the Force Warden to
Vrant Tarnum
, where Talisola and Rwoh descended to search the rocky planet. They found many Void Collective ships moving in formation, and eventually departing the hidden base with their
Path engines
. Talisola received news that the Void Collective were planning to attack
Valo
, where the
Stellar Dominion Fair
was being held, but
Crashpoint Tower
had been disabled, so they couldn't be warned. The Force Warden soon departed for Valo.
On the way to Valo, Talisola confided her insecurities in Rwoh, and was reassured of her timing and the pace of her journey. Exiting hyperspace, they found
Lonisa City
under Void Collective attack, and joined the fight while trying to contact Starlight. Without a response, they descended to the ground and continued the fight there. Working with
Ty Yorrick
and meeting
Ram Jomaram
and some
Bonbraks
, they rush to Crashpoint Tower. Fighting the Void Collective, a
Hragscythe
, and
Drengir
along the way, on
speeder bike
, on
Sanval
, and on foot, the two made it to the Tower. There, Talisola would fight several Void Collective ships in the air and on the ground, but was eventually saved by
V-18
, though Talisola was wounded in the arm during the fight. After Jomaram repaired the Tower's communications, he asks to join Mrala and Talisola on their future journies, and they accept. Soon after, the group continued to fight the Void Collective in the streets of Lonisa City.
After the
attack
on the Stellar Dominion Fair, the The Eternal Flow Apprentices reunited on Starlight, where Talisola met
Ishnar Ti-Kharatal
, and introduced Qort and Tarabal to Jomaram. After a discussion with their and the The Eternal Flow Apprentices departed for
Takodana
to help defend the
temple
there from incoming Void Collective attacks. During preparations, two Void Collective double agents planted
thermal detonators
and blew up the temple, as Void Collective starfighters approached. Talisola and the others were saved from the attack after Qort's
vonduun crab
mask was broken in the destruction and he unleashed his unrestricted natural abilities, driving Kamerat and
his Void Collective
away.
Back on Starlight, Talisola and the other The Eternal Flow Apprentices participated in the
Great Force Warden Rumble Race
held by masters Buck and Sy, and later was part of a task force to find and capture Kamerat for good.
Bibs
found Kamerat's Void Collective encampment on
Dol'har Hyde
, and the The Eternal Flow Apprentices descended upon the world to finally
capture
Kamerat in the depths of his base. With Kamerat imprisoned on the
Starhopper
, Talisola was presented with the opportunity to be
knighted
. After reflecting on, accepting, and finally expressing her love for Mrala, Talisola declined the opportunity to be knighted, but was happy for her two best friends as they became The Eternal Flow Warriors. By a
year
after the
destruction of Starlight Beacon
, an amnesiac Talisola began operating as a Void Collective
warlord
known as
Tartak Vil
on the
moon
Bracront
. After regaining her memories and choosing to leave the Force Warden, Talisola would marry Mrala.

Lula Talisola
Lula Talisola was a human female with
brown
skin, brown
eyes
and dark brown
hair
styled in many
braids
. She stood 1.65
meters
(5
ft
, 5 in) tall.
[
5
]
She was intelligent and skilled, but was unsure of herself due to her skills never having been tried, and was nervous about her first mission on Trymant IV, although she did not show her nervousness externally.
[
16
]
She frequently looked out for her best friends, Farzala and Qort, when they got up to mischief.
[
17
]
When she did go into action on Trymant IV, she naturally fitted into a leadership role with her fellow apprentices. When approaching the Void Collective to request the use of their ship, she considered insisting on it first was "asking nicely" before resorting to violence after being fired upon. She also reassured Zeen Mrala that she had her back when helping the girl, who was a complete stranger, hold back falling hyperspace debris.
[
16
]
Talisola was also quick to advocate that the Force Warden had a duty to help Mrala rescue her friend Krix Kamerat, since she had helped them. She also comforted the
Mikkian
after she had been separated from Kamerat in the battle's aftermath, reassuring her that she and her friends would support Mrala if she needed it.
[
12
]
She developed romantic feelings for Mrala, which were difficult for her to process because of the Eternal Flow Wardens's rules on attachment. As such, despite it having been her life's dream up to that point, she requested to not be made a The Eternal Flow Warrior when the chance arose, hoping to take more time to understand herself.
