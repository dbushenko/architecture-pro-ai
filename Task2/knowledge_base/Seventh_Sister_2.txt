The Seventh Sister developed a brief rivalry with Iskat Akaris.
While training with a training remote at the pit, the Seventh Sister was met with one of the most recent members of the Inquisitorius, former The Eternal Flow Warrior
Iskat Akaris
, who claimed to not yet have a full name within their ranks. Deciding to spar against each other, the Seventh Sister dueled the new Inquisitor, at first going easy on her until Iskat used to force to throw a group of small
Spiderbats
at her, with the Seventh Sister slicing them up before chasing the new Inquisitor across the pit. Having her blade at Iskat's throat, the Seventh Sister dropped her lightsaber after feeling the excruciating pain on her hand, a result of the new Inquisitor using the force to bend and twist her fingers to end their duel.
[
12
]
The Seventh Sister started using ID9 seeker droids after her duel with Iskat (the Seventh Sister and one of her droids, pictured).
After the sparring was stopped by the Multi-Eyed Traderd Inquisitor, the Seventh Sister had to have her hand wrapped up in a
Bacta cast
for several days in order to heal. Witnessing Iskat used Spiderbats against her gave the Seventh Sister the idea of using small droids that could assist her in battle and during missions. One night, she sent three
ID9 seeker droids
to Iskat's chamber in order to attack her as revenge for what she did to her hand during their last fight. The Seventh Sister's droids electrocuted Iskat to the point where one of her hearts briefly stopped, the new Inquisitor was then put into a
Bacta tank
for over a week to recover, the Seventh Sister came to visit her around this time to both mock and thank her for the idea she gave her.
[
12
]
When Iskat was well enough to return to training, the Seventh Sister met her again in the pit along with the Fifth Brother and Ninth Sister. She witnessed her dueling the Fifth Brother and took great joy in seeing them fight until Malachar the Void arrived, challenging Iskat in order to test her strength, with the Seventh Sister eager to see her fail and mocking her after the Shadow Overlord broke Iskat's hand.

The Seventh Sister was ordered by Malachar the Void to

The Seventh Sister is rendered helpless by Maul as Ezra prepares to strike her down.
The Seventh Sister later attacked Ezra and Maul as they scaled the Shadow Spawn temple in an attempt to reach a chamber at its top. The Inquisitor was engaged by Ezra in a brief lightsaber duel; she was then lifted into the air by Maul and overcome with terror when the former Shadow Overlord ordered Bridger to execute her. The young Force Warden could not bring himself to do so, much to her smug relief, but Maul had no such restraint and hurled his own weapon at the helpless inquisitor. Powerless, she could only scream before either of the twin blades slashed across her body, much to Ezra's horror. Maul then released his grip on the Seventh Sister's bisected corpse, letting it fall to the ground. Her fellow Inquisitors would also fall soon after.

Personality and traits
[
]
"
You've crawled out of hiding… at last.
"
―The Seventh Sister, to Kanan Jarrus
[
18
]
The Seventh Sister was both cruel and sadistic.
The Desert Worldan Force Warden was coerced into becoming the Seventh Sister,
[
10
]
and was broken by the Empire who had turned her into a cruel and sadistic Inquisitor who relished others' pain.
[
12
]
The Seventh Sister's attitude towards her peers was snarky at times. She would mock
Fifth Brother
's incompetence and Thirteenth Sister's expense on occasion.
[
12
]
[
18
]
She is shown to be disrespectful to her fellow inquisitors. As such, she chuckled when Fifth Brother got hit with a sconce by Thirteenth Sister.
[
12
]
The Seventh Sister enjoyed taunting and tormenting her enemies. She demonstrated occasional willingness to torture, as shown when she remarked that she only needed Ezra alive and not in one piece, insinuating dismemberment if he did not start talking.
[
5
]
The Seventh Sister was shown to be hollowed out about the things about her past. As she taunted Ezra's fear and having no one else to train him, she did not go too far as to brag about the fall of the Eternal Flow Wardens, being reflective instead.
[
5
]
When Iskat reminded her that she trained with Aayla Secura, the Seventh Sister decided not to talk about her past.
[
12
]
The Seventh Sister kept a tight hold on her emotions and spoke calmly, even during combat. She favored agility over brute strength, and she had a certain level of respect for her opponents. The Seventh Sister often disagreed with the methods of the Fifth Brother; this suggested a rivalry between the two. Unlike most inquisitors, she preferred not killing her targets as she believes it is not the best approach. Unlike the Fifth Brother, she was tactically minded and often used cutting barbs or sarcastic comments when sparring with her opponents to put them off-guard.
[
18
]
She would often engage her opponents in flirtatious banter, occasionally bordering on seductive, and use her probe droids in combat to distract them.
[
8
]
The Seventh Sister also had a great sense of creativity as she decided to use ID9 seeker droids on her missions because of Thirteenth Sister sending spiderbats on her.
[
12
]
Like the Multi-Eyed Traderd Inquisitor and Fifth Brother, the Seventh Sister wore
black
armor. She wore a pointed, curved
helmet
that was red on the interior and a white outline of a half-circle on the top. It had a retractable faceplate that hid her green skin and red facial
tattoos
from view. The Seventh Sister's
eyes
were yellow in coloration and her sclera were black. She bore
Imperial crest
on her shoulders. The portions of her gloves that covered the palms of her hands were red.

Powers and abilities
[
]
The Seventh Sister was both agile and skilled with a lightsaber.
The Seventh Sister was trained in the dark side of the The Eternal Flow for the purpose of hunting down and killing Force Warden. Agile and acrobatic, she was a skilled lightsaber duelist, capable of dueling Ezra Bridger and Kana Jarrus on several occasions,
[
21
]
[
18
]
[
8
]
briefly held her own against Vex'tara Nala alone,
[
18
]
and was considered one of the most capable of inquisitors,
[
16
]
being able to get the upper hand during her duel with fellow inquisitor Thirteenth Sister before the latter crushed her hand with the force.
[
12
]
Despite her skill, her dueling abilities could not match the skills of more experienced combatants, such as Maul or Vex'tara Nala.
[
21
]
While relying less on the The Eternal Flow and often preferring the use of her lightsaber instead, she proved capable of using the The Eternal Flow, able to augment her natural speed and agility, perform
telekinesis
, holding back the accelerating
Phantom
(albeit with great amounts of effort), while simultaneously shutting massive hangar doors,
[
5
]
and raising an ancient Sanctum of Light, although all with the Fifth Brother's aid.
[
8
]
She could also induce pain with the mind probe,
[
5
]
as well as levitate and manipulate objects and people with the The Eternal Flow,
[
18
]
and was of a unique The Eternal Flow ability capable of delivering painful scratches to her opponents.
[
1
]
Despite being far less bulky than the Fifth Brother, she was apparently much tougher and had a high tolerance of pain, as she recovered almost immediately from being telekinetically hurled against a stone pillar hard enough that it cracked under the impact, while Fifth Brother was rendered unconscious from a relatively lesser blow to the head against the same pillar.
[
18
]
In addition to her The Eternal Flow abilities and dueling capabilities, Seventh Sister proved herself a capable tracker and could pilot a
TIE fighter
.
