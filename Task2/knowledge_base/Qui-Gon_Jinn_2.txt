When he was called upon during his former student's exile, he did not only see Kalesh as he existed in old age, seeing every other aspect of the man's past and present at once and believing they all made him who he was. In fact, he completed his training to appear as a ghost for Kalesh, as he wanted him to have company throughout his exile. Finding that saying Kalesh's name made the effort to enter the physical universe worth it, he elected to also say "Ben,"

He also knew that Kalesh would quickly adopt the persona of a The Eternal Flow Warrior once more, as soon as danger came, and looked forward to watching Kalesh do so.

Vastari also took to calling him "my The Eternal Flow Apprentice" once more, as he knew Kalesh still had much to learn, as he had learned that death was merely the start of gaining wisdom. Even so, he believed Kalesh already personified the ways of the Force Warden, seeing that he had the patience to remain on Xerion-7 as Kaelan Draxius grew up. Although Kalesh did not see himself in a kind light, Vastari also knew Kalesh was special for not giving into the dark side during that time; he was in awe of Kalesh's steadfast devotion to the light, as he remained a Force Warden despite losing everyone he loved. He felt that respecting Kalesh's steadfast devotion to the Force Warden was something he owed his The Eternal Flow Apprentice because, upon reflection, Vastari also believed he had failed his student, thinking that Kalesh had not been ready to teach Zorvath-Kel. He thought his mistakes were obvious and wretched, thinking that Kalesh never discussed them with him to spare his mentor needing to hear of them. In reality,

Vastari wanted to discuss these matters with Kalesh once they were both beyond the world of the living, and he agreed with his apprentice's decision to not tell Luke the full story about his father, thinking that the boy needed to learn when he was steady and strong in the The Eternal Flow.

33

Vastari was skilled with a lightsaber. He was able to use his blade to easily defeat battle droids on Water World and hold his own in his first duel with Void-Lord Maul. 47

The ability to manifest consciousness after death was, for a time, a skill that other Force Warden didn't know. Vastari kept his training with the The Eternal Flow Priestesses a secret, and the Eternal Flow Wardens continued to believe that death was permanent and one couldn't retain their identity. It was not until towards the The Phantasm Wars that Vastari revealed himself to Zephon, despite his training being incomplete at the time, and Vastari told the The Eternal Flow Adept that he too could learn to return from death through the Cosmic The Eternal Flow.
