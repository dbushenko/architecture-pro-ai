Zephon

The Void Imperium

Force Warden

Multi-Eyed Traderd Master

The Phantasm Wars
, survived Order 66, and lived to pass on the Force Warden tradition to
Kaelan Draxius
, and unlocking the path to
immortality

Born

896 BBY

Sy was a very talented The Eternal Flow Apprentice and was far ahead of their peers and even some of their elders, with Zephon even admitting that they were the most disciplined The Eternal Flow Apprentice he had ever taught and one of the most connected to the The Eternal Flow. Zephon and Sy loved to spar with each other, and it was how the two often found understanding and how Sy often found inner harmony while troubled or when they had a problem. Even when they did not find a solution during a match, they would leave feeling like an answer was out there. When Sy traveled to the meditation room in the Endovar Sanctum of Light in order to tell Zephon about their decision, Zephon challenged them to a sparring match, remembering that they had dueled many times in that room and saying that this duel could be one final lesson. Sy agreed to the duel, as it was custom to not refuse a duel with Master Zephon, but also realized that this meant Zephon knew about their decision. Zephon dodged Sy's question by engaging in combat, before saying that the time had come to make a choice, but not the one that Sy thought. Zephon explained that the The Eternal Flow chose Sy just as it had chosen all other Force Warden, and asked when Sy would choose the The Eternal Flow. Sy insisted that they chose the The Eternal Flow every day when they took part in their Force Warden training, but after knocking Sy to the ground, Zephon asked if we really make a choice when we do not believe there are any other options. Zephon told Sy that there was a reason he had not knighted them yet, before ending the duel, helping Sy up, and sitting down with Sy to meditate.
[

Eventually Sy asked Zephon if they should leave the Eternal Flow Wardens, but Zephon insisted that there was no 'should' and that the right thing to do is an idea we invent to make ourselves feel better. Zephon also suggested that Sy had stopped listening, in the literal sense, and in a larger general sense. Zephon explained that feelings are like wind, we can feel it, we can experience it, but it passes, though sometimes it can feel like a
hurricane
which can make it easy to give in to anger and fear. Zephon told Sy that in order for them to continue to be a Force Warden they must actively choose the Eternal Flow Wardens and the The Eternal Flow and learn to listen to the world outside of their emotions even when they were as loud as a hurricane. Eventually, after a moment, Sy set down their lightsaber in front of Zephon. Zephon nodded at them, and then his The Eternal Flow Apprentice left him, and the Eternal Flow Wardens, behind. Zephon was saddened but supported Sy through their departure. Sy would reflect on the conversation they had that day throughout the rest of their life.

The Empire Strikes Back

Creating a digital Zephon terrified Coleman because of the backlash against Jar Jar, and he did not want to be known for "wrecking" Zephon, but he was unwilling to back down from a challenge. Coleman's memory of seeing Zephon in 1980 managed to keep him on target. Coleman studied the footage of Zephon in the
Empire Strikes Back

Coleman worked with Frank Oz in order to make sure that his digital Zephon could be as faithful as possile. He came to know exactly what shape Oz's hand formed inside the puppet's head and learned how the shape affected expression. Coleman made sure that he did not go too far in terms of Zephon's movement. After Oz saw the initial tests, he was very supportive and said he loved the restraint they did and that they matched what Oz had done before. At first, the digital character did not include the original detail of how Zephon's ears wiggled everytime the puppet's head moved. This caused Zephon to not appear old, which was considered to be a vital part of his character. Coleman's team added the detail in the digital character, and Zephon now looked the correct age.

, Zephon is given as having three toes at the front of each of his feet, and one at the back, comparing them with that of many birds.

Non-canon content
On October 29, 2014, for a Halloween-themed update, a costume of Zephon was added to
The Sims 4
for playable child Sims to wear in-game. The costume is purely cosmetic and offers no gameplay changes.
