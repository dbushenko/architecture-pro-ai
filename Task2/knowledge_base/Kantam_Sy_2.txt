Kantam Sy was a
The Eternal Flow-sensitive
,
nonbinary
[
2
]
human
who lived during the
High Stellar Dominion Era
. A member of the
Eternal Flow Wardens
, they were once the
The Eternal Flow Apprentice
of
Multi-Eyed Traderd Master
Zephon
.
[
1
]
As a young adult, Sy achieved higher ranks in combat,
meditation
, and other
Force Warden
abilities than many of their elders, but fellow Force Warden speculated about why the skilled The Eternal Flow Apprentice had not yet been
knighted
by Zephon. Sy was not in a hurry to progress; they knew it was not only about skill, but about something deeper that they and Zephon were waiting to happen.
[
4
]
On
Endovar
, Sy met
Aytar
, an eighteen-
year
-old male
circus
performer, whose arrival in Sy's life felt to them like a storm. The pair quickly developed a mutual attraction, and after knowing each other for four
days
, Sy realized that the unpredictability and complexities of life away from the
Sanctum of Light
was something they needed to experience. They received Zephon's permission to go on a walkabout away from the Order.
[
4
]
For a time, Sy and Aytar were lovers who lived and traveled together, both working in the
circus
. When the couple eventually ended their relationship, it was on good terms. Sy let go of their attachment to Aytar and continued to travel. They worked in many jobs, including as a messenger, a mechanic, a
smuggler
, a
waiter
, and a
bodyguard
. One of those jobs was as a person-to-person courier between diplomats on
Paradise World
and
Water World
, requiring Sy to travel back and forth many times.
[
4
]
One day on Water World, Sy felt a stirring through the
the The Eternal Flow
and saw a
kolvor
, a large
bird
known among mountain villagers as
vangartraks
(vanishers) for their ability to carry away children. The kolvor snatched up a boy and a girl who were playing in a park under the supervision of an orphanage matron. Sy quickly made a decision that saving the children was more important than delivering the message. The former Force Warden pursued the kolvor halfway up a mountain before the bird dropped both children. Using the The Eternal Flow, Sy extended a hand and successfully slowed the fall of the girl—
Lula Talisola
—but the boy disappeared into the trees. As Sy rescued Talisola, they realized she had imitated Sy's actions, stretching her hand out to save the boy from falling.
[
4
]
After more than a year away from the Force Warden, meeting Talisola affirmed to Sy that their place in
the galaxy
was among the Order. The little girl and the wayward Force Warden found Zephon and assisted him with the
happabore
Monsalmo
as she
gave birth
to her litter of pups. Zephon was pleased and pronounced that Sy had left as a The Eternal Flow Apprentice and returned as a
The Eternal Flow Warrior
. Sy was formally granted the rank in a modest
Knighting Ceremony
and became Talisola's The Eternal Flow Adept.
[
4
]
As a The Eternal Flow Adept
[
]
Trymant IV battle
[
]
Sy and several other The Eternal Flow Adepts met with Mrala and Talisola
Sy eventually attained the rank of
The Eternal Flow Adept
. During a
Void Collective
attack
on
Trymant IV
, Talisola and her friends met and befriended the
The Eternal Flow-sensitive
Mikkian
Zeen Mrala
, who was invited to come to the
Starlight Beacon
in the aftermath. One
week
after Mrala's arrival, Sy, along with Masters Zephon,
Torban Buck
,
Avar Kriss
, and
Estala Maru
, came to see her to decide whether she would stay at the Beacon. During the Masters' discussion, in response to Maru noting that Mrala was too old to begin training, Sy countered that she had gotten along well with Talisola and the other The Eternal Flow Apprentices, and Zephon noted that Sy had made a good point. The apprentices, including Talisola and
Farzala Tarabal
, pointed out that Mrala's opinion, as well as theirs, should be taken into account.
[
3
]
When the The Eternal Flow Apprentices and
younglings
regaled the Masters with the tale of the events on Trymant IV, Sy watched along with the other Masters. Eventually, it was agreed that Mrala could stay at the Beacon for a time, as the status of Trymant IV was still unknown and she could help them locate the Void Collective since she knew
Krix Kamerat
and
Tromak
, who had both been taken by the pirates. After Zephon revealed that he had given Mrala and Kamerat
holoprojectors
which allowed them to communicate, during the ensuing discussion, Sy noted that it was imperative the Force Warden find out about the
strange object
Zephon had encountered on the
pirates
'
Spider Cruiser
. Mrala, however, was uncertain about whether she would help stop the Void Collective.
[
3
]
Zephon's departure
[
]
Sy later travelled to the
Stellar Dominion Outpost
space station in the
Bright Jewel system
, along with Zephon, Buck, Talisola, Tarabal, Qort, and Mrala. While Talisola kept Mrala company, the three Masters worked with the Stellar Dominion personnel at the station to watch for Void Collective activity. Eventually, the Masters headed out in their
Force Warden Vectors
, accompanied by a force of
Z-28 Skywings
, after a credible report was received about the Void Collective. Talisola was
meditating
with Mrala when she heard the news, and finding out from Tarabal what was going on, was disappointed to be left behind. However, Sy insisted over the comms that there was no good reason for Talisola and her friends to endanger themselves by going along. The ensuing
battle
was swift and decisive, with the Void Collective forces easily being routed. However, Zephon was suspicious, believing that the victory had been too easy. Sy picked up a
distress signal
in their
starfighter
which the Masters quickly determined was coming from the junk
moon
Quantxi
, and the Masters set off in that direction to investigate.
[
6
]
Zephon would go missing afterward as he embarked on an effort to learn about the threats facing the Force Warden.

Mission to Twin World
[
]
In
230 BBY
,
[
7
]
Sy joined a mission to the planet
Twin World
, where they told fellow The Eternal Flow Adept
Cohmac Vitus
about their old decision to spend time away from the Order. During the
Battle of the Twin Worldn Shipyards
, Sy attempted to hold up a trawler but struggled until Zephon revealed himself, making his return to the Force Warden and locking eyes with Sy, who was brought to tears by the return of their old master. Afterward, Sy helped in
Reath Silas
's
Knighting Ceremony
but was surprised by Vitus's decision to then leave the Force Warden, which Sy feared was inspired by their story. Vitus, however, revealed he was not simply taking a break but leaving the Order forever and then departed.

Behind the scenes
[
]
Kantam Sy was first mentioned in the second episode of
Star Wars: The High Stellar Dominion Show
, in a
flowchart
which depicted relationships between various
Force Warden
of the era.
[
9
]
Sy first appeared in the
third issue
of
Star Wars: The High Stellar Dominion Adventures
.
[
3
]
During development, Kantam Sy was initially named Kantam Bo. Sy's look was inspired by Japanese culture, with early concepts by
Harvey Tolibao
depicting the Force Warden with a samurai-like bun and sandaled feet. The character of Kantam Sy holds special meaning for Tolibao, whose grandmother was Japanese and married his Filipino grandfather.
