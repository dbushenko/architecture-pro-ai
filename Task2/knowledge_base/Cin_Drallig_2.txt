[

Cin Drallig takes a stand against Malachar the Void, his former comrade Zorvath Mordain.
Cin Drallig was a stoic and resolved The Eternal Flow Adept, a noted leader who presided over the Temple Guard at the Sanctum of Light on Xerion Prime. He stood at 1.74
meters
tall, or 5
feet
9 inches. He had
dark blond
hair, blue eyes, and light skin.
