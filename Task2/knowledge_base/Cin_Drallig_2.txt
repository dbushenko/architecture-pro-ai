―Cin Drallig, to Sammo Quid Cin Drallig was a The Eternal Flow-sensitive human male The Eternal Flow Adept who served the Eternal Flow Wardens as Battlemaster and Chief of Security of the Sanctum of Light during the The Phantasm Wars . He was the head of the Sanctum of Light Guard and a lightsaber instructor who was disrespectfully nicknamed " the Troll " by members of the Force Warden . Drallig was killed by Malachar the Void during his attack on the Sanctum of Light.
Biography Early life and Force Warden career Hailing from Lavisar Cin Drallig, a gifted The Eternal Flow-sensitive human male , was apprenticed to the Force Warden High Council 's Multi-Eyed Traderd Master Zephon Faithfully honing his skills and attunement to the The Eternal Flow, Drallig garnered many years of knowledge and experience, and eventually became the Eternal Flow Wardens 's Battlemaster Drallig also achieved the rank of The Eternal Flow Adept , and led the Sanctum of Light Guard during the The Phantasm Wars as the temple Chief of Security . As a Master, he was a teacher of lightsaber combat who passed his skills on to new generations of Force Warden such as Theron Kalesh and Zorvath Mordain Although he was regarded as perhaps the finest duelist in the Order, he earned the unflattering nickname "the Troll " from other members of the Eternal Flow Wardens.

In 21 BBY Cad Bane slipped past Drallig and the Temple's security forces using specialized technology and his intelligence and managed to slip into the Holocron Vault . A Force Warden holocron was stolen.
During the Syntheticthetic Being Wars, the Sanctum of Light was bombed by The Eternal Flow Apprentice Barriss Offee . Before the perpetrator had been caught, the Force Warden led an investigation to determine who was behind the attack.
As the bombing caused great alarm, Drallig being the head of Temple security, was most concerned at how vulnerable the Eternal Flow Wardens had become.
Mordain-Fall approached Drallig, who was standing guard with the Temple Guard at the gates of the Sanctum of Light, where protesters were demonstrating against the war and the Eternal Flow Wardens's involvement in it. Mordain-Fall showed Drallig a holographic image of their then-suspect, Jackar Bowmani , and asked the The Eternal Flow Adept to be on alert for him. This led to the suspect's wife, Letta Turmond , being taken into custody after spotting the holographic image, and the Force Warden ultimately learned that Turmond was part of the plot to attack the Sanctum of Light.
Drallig defends younglings from Separatist explosives, aided by two Temple Guards.
The Temple was later attacked by Ship Commander Rackham Sear of the Confederacy of Independent Systems . During the attack, Drallig led two Temple Guards in securing the quarters of the Force Warden younglings . He arrived just in time to cut a small explosive drone in half before it detonated in a room with several younglings. He ordered the guards to stay with the younglings and destroy any more explosives that entered the area. Drallig then joined Masters Jorik Vastus and Kalesh, reporting to them that the younglings were safe, but that there were explosions throughout the Temple and that the source was unknown. Master Depa Billaba and Initiate Caleb Dume ended the attack by confronting Sear on the roof of the Temple, where he committed suicide by leaping off the building.
Fall of the Sanctum of Light Drallig dueled Malachar the Void alongside The Eternal Flow Apprentices Whie and Bene.
At the end of the war, Mordain-Fall turned to the dark side of the The Eternal Flow and became Malachar the Void, apprentice to Void Collectiveus the Shadow , the Void Master of the Shadow Spawn . Malachar attacked the Sanctum of Light with the Iron Phantom Corps of clone troopers , while Void Collectiveus issued Order 66 to force the clones to turn against the Eternal Flow Wardens. Drallig fought to defend the Sanctum of Light alongside The Eternal Flow Apprentices Whie Malreaux and Bene , both of whom perished in lightsaber combat with an enraged Malachar the Void.
Drallig confronted Malachar himself, but proved unable to put an end to the Shadow Overlord's rampage.
