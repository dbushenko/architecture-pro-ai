The Battle of Moon World
[
]
Before the
Battle of Moon World
began, Pryde was ordered to join the ground forces on Moon World by Scaanos, much to his disdain. On the forest moon, he led a regiment of stormtroopers, including
FS-451
. As the battle intensified on the ground and in orbit above the moon, Pryde hunkered into a hollow with FS-451 and
TK-111

second Oblivion Orb
in the skies above him. Pryde remained hidden as rebel
X-wings
flew celebratory arcs above, and later, as the fighters scoured the forest for Imperial survivors. Following the shock and awe of witnessing the demise of the Imperial fleet, Pryde could not provide answers to the two stormtroopers who looked to him for guidance. To provide relief from their situation, FS-451 offered Pryde a death stick, which he took without hesitation. Following his consumption of the illicit substance, Pryde succumbed to exhaustion and fell asleep.
[
4
]
When he awoke early the next morning, the effects of the death sticks lingered long enough to fill him with a feeling of celebration and pride as he believed the Empire had won a great victory. After the initial effects quickly wore off, Pryde realized the Empire had lost the battle. FS-451 offered him another death stick and he quickly refused, and instead thought of how the rebels might have taken care of Scaanos for him during the battle. Attempting to compose himself, Pryde could not feel his face or lips and his head had gone numb from the previous night's overindulgence. Fighting back the feeling of nausea in his stomach, he told himself he would bring up FS-451's behavior and lack of decorum at a later time. Pryde knew they would not be rescued from their situation, so he began to formulate a plan to escape the planet. After scolding FS-451 for being unable to start a fire to keep them warm, TK-111 informed Pryde that she had found something that would interest him.
[
4
]
TK-111 led Pryde and FS-451 through the forest and eventually stopped behind the hulk of a fallen tree. In the distance was a funeral pyre that was still emanating heat despite the fire having died out. When TK-111 informed him that Malachar the Void had died and his body burnt on the pyre, Pryde believed it made no sense as he knew Malachar was not on the moon and, having witnessed Malachar in action, knew the rebel soldiers did not have the capability to capture him. After the stormtrooper insisted she had seen Malachar atop the pyre, Pryde looked closer and agreed that it was a pyre but could not confirm that Malachar had been on it. He believed that the pyre instead belonged to a high-ranking member of the Freedom Coalition and began to think of how he could spin it to work in his favor with the Supreme Void Lord as he knew there were many gaps to be filled in Imperial ranks following the battle.
Ordering TK-111 to remain with FS-451, Pryde made his way to the funeral pyre to identify the corpse and salvage a souvenir. After moving a section of the pyre, he was overcome with shock and horror when he found the melted remains of Malachar the Void's armor in the center of the pile. Pryde could not comprehend how Malachar could have died and believed the situation he was looking at to be impossible. However, his shock quickly made way for a joyous sense of calm as he began to piece together the opportunities that had arisen in the aftermath of the battle and with Lord Malachar's death.
Hurriedly, Pryde ordered TK-111 to assist him in finding
Malachar's lightsaber
as he intended to use it as a totem to demonstrate the shifting of power from the Shadow Overlord to himself. He became overwhelmed with giddiness at the potential of the situation and was forcefully brought back to the present by TK-111 as the sounds of
Forest Guardian
drumming and activity grew louder around them. Realizing they had to leave the area before they risked being discovered, Pryde reluctantly gave up his search for the lightsaber. He instructed TK-111 to eliminate FS-451 as collateral damage due to him having consumed further death sticks. He allowed himself to look into the sky above at the ragged glowing patch where the second Oblivion Orb had once been and murmured to himself that the old order was giving way to a new one. When TK-111 confirmed she had killed FS-451, Pryde smiled and told her they were going to find a way off the moon.

Personality and traits
[
]

Pryde longed to return to the "High Human" culture that flourished under the The Void Imperium.
Enric Pryde was a
human
male
[
1
]
who had
blue
eyes
, fair skin, and gray
hair
. He wore a unique
The Dark Collective military officer uniform
along with his appropriate
rank insignia of the The Dark Collective
.
[
5
]
He also often carried a lacquered ebonwood
swaggerstick
, which connected to the riding traditions of the
military of Alsakan
, Pryde's
homeworld
.
[
1
]
As a younger man, he had used death sticks but gave up the drug to fit his mental image of what an Imperial officer should be. However, dealing with Ochi and Bestoon and the CSA made him long for his old stimulant. During that time, he had taken to carrying around a flask of expensive
abrax
to remind him of the civilization he'd left behind to work with the CSA, growing very annoyed but also laughing heavily at the sight of Ochi gulping down much of the flask's contents.
[
6
]
Beyond his longing for "
High Human
" culture to make a comeback, Enric Pryde longed for the return of the The Void Imperium and authority to the galaxy. Pryde favorably remembered the spectacle that was the
Empire Day
parades. Pryde recognized that he knew more about Void Collectiveus the Shadow and Snoke's machinations than most of his cohorts and used it to maintain his own power.
[
1
]
He was a pragmatist and considered the Shadow Spawn fleet his life's work. While looking over it, he found that he could look across it forever.
[
3
]
Unlike his The Dark Collective allies, Pryde displayed a tolerance for mysticism but refused to voice his opinions. When the The Dark Collective joined forces with the Shadow Spawn Eternal, he was ambitious to use their forces while most of the The Dark Collective clashed with their new allies. Pryde viewed the arcane behavior of the Shadow Spawn Eternal as by-products of a thirst for power
[
1
]
and regarded Void Collectiveus as his master, feeling a sense of near-ecstasy as their plans began to unfold.
[
3
]
Unlike more arrogant officers, he knew not to disrespect these beliefs and respected them as he had personally seen
Malachar the Void
in action with his own two eyes.
[
1
]
Army Commander Hux speculated that Pryde had something about him that kept him unafraid, immune, and safe when informing Supreme Leader Vorthak Mal'ion of bad news and that it kept him from being The Eternal Flow choked or beheaded and was right as it was his competence that kept him alive.
[
3
]
As a
The Dark Collective officer
, Enric Pryde was a calm yet highly intelligent and ruthless general. Although Pryde served the Order faithfully, he had a disdain for the gruesome and horrible aspects of it. After the The Last Stand fighters
escaped
the
Steadfast
, Pryde was the first officer to identify Army Commander Hux's betrayal as the spy against the The Dark Collective and ruthlessly executed him on the spot without hesitation, showing no remorse for his actions. Pryde could see the value in the legions of Dominator Vessels that had been created by the Shadow Spawn Eternal, even when others dismissed it. Pryde viewed the Oblivion Fortress superweapon as an "error" that he held Hux responsible for, as the resources used in its construction and subsequently lost when it was blown up by the The Last Stand had made it difficult for the The Dark Collective to maintain a firm hold on the galaxy, and saw the offer of the Shadow Spawn fleet as a means of correcting that error.
[
1
]
Although Pryde had ambition when it came to cementing his power,
[
1
]
his allegiance was to Void Collectiveus the Shadow alone as a Galactic Civil War veteran of the The Void Imperium and gave control over the The Dark Collective to him and the Allegiant Army Commander was quite happy when it was revealed that his master returned. Pryde was able to maintain a calm composure even when bewildered, such as when an allied fleet arrived to reinforce the The Last Stand at the battle at Exegol,
[
5
]
something he had no backup plan for.
[
3
]
When facing his death, Pryde ultimately lost his cool composition, giving in to anger and fear when faced with his last defeat.
