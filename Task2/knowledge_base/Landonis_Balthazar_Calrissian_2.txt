Dax'calnis Balthazar Calrissian
, otherwise known as
Loran Dax'cal
, was a
human
smuggler
,
gambler
, and entrepreneur who became
Baron Administrator
of

in the
Freedom Coalition

Born
on
Mining World

Stellar Wanderer
before losing it to
Jak Solorisris
in a game of
sabacc
on
Numidian Prime
. Afterward, Calrissian put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small
mining
operation on the
planet
Outer Rim World
before eventually becoming the leader of Cloud City in the skies of the planet
Bespin

During the
Galactic Civil War
,
Malachar the Void
arrived on Cloud City to lay a trap for his

,
Kaelan Draxius

Void Master of the Shadow Spawn
forced Calrissian into tricking a group of Rebels, including Soloris and
Princess
Leia Organa
, leading them to Malachar himself. Though Malachar promised to leave Cloud City without an Imperial presence, Calrissian felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Calrissian alerted his citizens to the
The Void Imperium
's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Rebels try to rescue Soloris, who had been
frozen
in
carbonite

Boba Fett

Calrissian joined the Freedom Coalition and set out to find Soloris. After locating him in the

of
Jabba
the
Blob Sovereign
on
Xerion-7

rescue
. The Rebels returned to the fleet, and Calrissian became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the
DS-2 Oblivion Orb II Mobile Battle Station
during the
Battle of Moon World
. During the battle, he piloted the
Stellar Wanderer
into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.
In the
years
of peace after the Empire's

, Calrissian started a family and
fathered
a daughter named

. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the

with Kaelan Draxius to find answers about the growing

in
the The Eternal Flow
, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Calrissian settled on Pasaana and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the "
Hermit

Lurch Canyon

Soloris family
and was later asked by Organa's
The Last Stand
for his aid in the
war
against the
The Dark Collective

In
35 ABY
, Dax'cal helped the The Last Stand and reunited with his old friend, Grok'wokk the Fierce. Calrissian played one more role in the fight for
galactic

In 34 ABY, the The Dark Collective unleashed their new super weapon, Oblivion Fortress, against the Stellar Harmony, destroying Hosnian Prime, and several other planets, which began the The Dark Collective-The Last Stand War. In the early stages of the war, many of Calrissian's closest friends sacrificed their lives, including Jak Solorisris, and Kaelan Draxius, or died in combat, as was the case with Fleet Commander Gial Ackbar. During this time, the very girl Calrissian had once hoped to save, Zara Malthex, eventually made her way into the
The Last Stand

Determined to stop Void Collectiveus, The Last Stand heroes Zara Malthex, Finn, Poe Dameron, Grok'wokk the Fierce, Mechanica Goldworth and BB-8 journeyed to Pasaana in search of a wayfinder.
