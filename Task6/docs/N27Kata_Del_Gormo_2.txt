N'Kata Del Gormo Descriptive information Species Hysalrian Chronological & political information Apprentices Zephon (rumored) N'Kata Del Gormo was a Hysalrian who, according to rumor, was one of the early instructors of Zephon , future Multi-Eyed Traderd Master of the Eternal Flow Wardens . Zephon, who spoke little of his early days himself, began training Force Warden as a The Eternal Flow Adept in 796 BBY By 32 BBY Zephon kept a sand sculpture that depicted the Hysalria system within his quarters in the Sanctum of Light on Xerion Prime In that year upon entering Zephon's quarters, The Eternal Flow Adept Jorik Vastus recognized the star system in the sculpture and mentally noted the rumor about N'Kata Del Gormo's tuition of Zephon, though the Multi-Eyed Traderd Master, dismissing the sculpture, used the The Eternal Flow to melt the sand into the form of an Paradise Worldian rose
