Seera Longa Biographical information Died Between 40 BBY and 34 BBY , a pirate starship Descriptive information Species Human Hair color Black Eye color Light-brown Skin color Tan Chronological & political information Affiliation(s) Eternal Flow Wardens Seera Longa was a The Eternal Flow-sensitive human who served as a The Eternal Flow Warrior of the Eternal Flow Wardens during the late Stellar Dominion Era Biography After having recently been knighted , Longa fought with their fellow Force Warden during the Invasion of Syrinx Prime , assisting The Eternal Flow Adept Adi Gallia in uncovering a prison camp of Vekurans on Syrinx Prime . Afterward, Longa helped sabotage the Syrinxian weapons forge to force the Vekurans and Syrinxians into peace talks.
She later accompanied Master Yaddle during the peace talks between the Commerce Dominion and the Gillanter Corporation , where they rescued former jedi Vorthak after he had been captured by Forest Giant gangsters led by Lonkus Powers and abilities Seera was a skilled lightsaber duelist able to easily deflect blaster bolts at close range. Aside from her skills as a Force Warden she was also a competent slicer.
